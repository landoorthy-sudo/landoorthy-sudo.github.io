## About me

Hi, I'm Landoorthy. I studied statistics and am now doing a master's in data science at CUHK-Shenzhen.

I work where AI meets storytelling. I've taken Stanford's CS336 and built small LLM and agent projects, and I spent a season as an editor adapting Chinese web novels into English short dramas for an overseas platform, designing the prompt workflows that made it run. Off the clock, I write fiction: my serialized novels and fan works have reached hundreds of thousands of readers.

I care about good stories, honest products, and a life with room to breathe. My dog Ali mostly cares about walks, and my friend's cats Sigua and Nangua drop by for naps.
